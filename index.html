IEEEConference paper · 2025

An Efficient Deep Learning Framework for Diabetic Retinopathy Classification using Generative Data Augmentation and Knowledge Distillation

Tasnimul Mohammad Fahim, Sarika Bintey Sabur, Jawad Bin Alam, Tanzim Hossain Ayon, Shuvo Datta, Azwad Aziz, Amitabha Chakrabarty

2025 IEEE 7th International Conference on Sustainable Technologies for Industry 5.0 (STI), Dhaka, Bangladesh

Undergraduate thesis · Supervised by Dr. Amitabha Chakrabarty, BRAC University

Diabetic retinopathy is a leading cause of preventable blindness. We built a three-stage framework for automated screening: Progressive Growing GANs generate synthetic eye images to fix class imbalance, an EfficientNet-V2 and Vision Transformer ensemble classifies five severity levels, and knowledge distillation shrinks the model into a lightweight MobileNetV2 that can run in low-resource clinics.

Data Science · CSE437 research

Robust Bengali Digit Recognition

A study of how HOG+SVM, a custom CNN and MobileNetV2 hold up when handwritten Bengali digits are degraded by noise, blur, rotation and contrast loss, and whether data augmentation closes the gap.

  • Built with Shuvojit Banik. My part: HOG features, model training, evaluation and the augmentation study
  • MobileNetV2 led at 85.2%, but the most accurate, most stable and most fragile models were three different systems
  • Distortion-based augmentation lifted CNN accuracy under blur from 13% to 61%
