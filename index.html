Research

Published work

My undergraduate thesis, published at an IEEE international conference.

IEEEConference paper · 2025

An Efficient Deep Learning Framework for Diabetic Retinopathy Classification using Generative Data Augmentation and Knowledge Distillation

Tasnimul Mohammad Fahim, Sarika Bintey Sabur, Jawad Bin Alam, Tanzim Hossain Ayon, Shuvo Datta, Azwad Aziz, Amitabha Chakrabarty

2025 IEEE 7th International Conference on Sustainable Technologies for Industry 5.0 (STI), Dhaka, Bangladesh

Undergraduate thesis · Supervised by Dr. Amitabha Chakrabarty, BRAC University

Diabetic retinopathy is a leading cause of preventable blindness. We built a three-stage framework for automated screening: Progressive Growing GANs generate synthetic eye images to fix class imbalance, an EfficientNet-V2 and Vision Transformer ensemble classifies five severity levels, and knowledge distillation shrinks the model into a lightweight MobileNetV2 that can run in low-resource clinics.

  1. 1

    Balance the data

    A class-conditional PG-GAN adds 6,338 synthetic images to the 3,662 real ones: 2,000 per class.

  2. 2

    Train the teacher

    EfficientNet-V2-S (93.6%) and ViT-B/16 (93.1%) fused into an ensemble: 95.8% accuracy, 0.975 QWK.

  3. 3

    Distil the student

    MobileNetV2 learns from the teacher: 3.5M parameters, 14 MB, 61 ms per image.

Data Science · CSE437 research

Robust Bengali Digit Recognition

A study of how HOG+SVM, a custom CNN and MobileNetV2 hold up when handwritten Bengali digits are degraded by noise, blur, rotation and contrast loss, and whether data augmentation closes the gap.

  • Built with Shuvojit Banik. My part: HOG features, model training, evaluation and the augmentation study
  • MobileNetV2 led at 85.2%, but the most accurate, most stable and most fragile models were three different systems
  • Distortion-based augmentation lifted CNN accuracy under blur from 13% to 61%

Computer Interfacing · CSE360 lab project

Smart Waste Segregator

A low-cost smart bin that uses an on-device vision model to sort waste into biodegradable and non-biodegradable compartments, and monitors smoke and air quality inside the bin.

  • Group of four. My part: wiring and testing the gas sensors, and setting up the camera tripod
  • An ESP32-CAM runs an Edge Impulse detection model and sends results to an Arduino Uno over UART, which drives a servo to drop the item on the right side
  • Built for about 3,795 BDT. Limited by a low-resolution camera and a model that knows only two item types

Artificial Intelligence · CSE422 lab project

E-commerce Delivery Prediction

A binary classification study in the logistics domain: predicting whether an e-commerce order arrives on time or late, from exploratory analysis through to model evaluation.

  • Group of two. Uses 10 shipment and customer features from about 11,000 orders
  • Compared KNN, Decision Tree, Logistic Regression, Naive Bayes and a neural network, with 5-fold cross-validation
  • The neural network scored best at 67% test accuracy, but every model landed within a few points because the features carry only a weak signal
